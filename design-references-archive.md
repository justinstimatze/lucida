## Coverage summary

- **pushing-pixels.org/fui/**: 18 / 62 entries fetched in detail; remaining 44 enumerated by URL+title only. The fetched 18 cover every load-bearing voice (Coleran, Hojlund, Hansen, Sheldon-Hicks, GMUNK, McFetridge, Booth/BLIND, Bramall, LePore x2, Khairul Ahmed, Russell, Thorp, Fraser, Henri/Grimm Alien Earth, Taglianetti, Eszenyi, Stylow, Ho, Koltai, Hays, Lomax, McDermott).

Year-archives only show ~10 most recent posts each without paginated `/page/N/` access, and several pages 404 (`/category/movie/`, `/2011/`).

## Meta-analysis

### Recurring visual conventions across the corpus

**Palette**:
- **Cyan/blue dominant** as default-neutral. Lomax: "blue dominates sci-fi because audiences expect it." Bramall: "blue looks great next to skin tone" + dark backgrounds prevent on-set light-spill.
- **Red as alarm exclusively** — never decorative. Hojlund's "blue/cyan default; strategic red/green for warnings/status."
- **Amber/orange for warmth or alternative-faction**: SHIELD orange-or-blue mono-screens (extradiegetic palette discipline); LexOS oranges as "fluid frenetic" character signal; Avengers case-file greyscale-+-amber-arcs.
- **Per-character/faction palette systems** — load-bearing principle: LexOS clean white/grey/orange vs BatOS dark sinister (Ahmed); Kingsman military-green vs Valentine pink/purple (Booth); Lance blue / Killian red-black / Eyes cyan-pink (Koltai). Lucida analog: per-substrate or per-cell-genre palette tokens.
- **McFetridge's *Her* anti-FUI exception**: warm pastels, no scanlines, no holograms. Demonstrates that "futuristic" without "FUI" is achievable when the story is about technology *receding*.

**Typography**:
- All-caps technical sans for hero readouts.
- Mono + scanline-aesthetic for retro / CRT modes (Alien: Earth Maginot).
- Stark utilitarian sans for clinical-authority (Blade Runner).
- Hojlund/Hansen "micro-text" detail layer that's not meant to be read but rewards inspection.

**Motion patterns**:
- **Peripheral-to-center attention escalation** (Iron Man HUD): small icons bloom into hero elements when their data becomes load-bearing. Pattern repeats in Hojlund's hero/ambient split, GMUNK's "remove greeble," Hays's "good design, point comes across in a second."
- **State-confirming feedback animation**: switch-lights light up to confirm, blink-rate as urgency (PKE meter), purple-glow on activation (kimoyo beads). Always *visible* state.
- **Continuous redraw** for living-system feel: gauges animate when problems arise; activity-pulses on data traces (Hackers City of Text).
- **Volumetric expand from gesture**: ten-fingertips-thrown-outward expands portraits into ceiling-height 3D windows (Avengers case-file). Always physical→volumetric, never just "appears."
- **Audio-reactive composition** (Russell on Black Mirror w/ Houdini): generative motion driven by data signal, not keyframes.

**Compositional moves**:
- **Wireframes + reticles + biometrics** stacked translucently (Iron Man HUD).
- **Grid/keyline systems** under apparent free-form (Fraser, Sheldon-Hicks Martian's modular grid scaling wristwatch→9×3m wall).
- **Concentric ring nav** (McDermott Black Mirror Entire-History-of-You; Doctor Strange Tibet-mode chapter-ring+rotating-saucer). Maps to clock/eye/tree-ring intuitions.
- **Worn-tactile-character imperfection** (Guardians "scratched glass with misty fuzziness"; Alien: Earth Maginot CRT bulge). Anti-sterile-futurism.
- **Isometric cityscape-as-data** (Hackers, Johnny Mnemonic City of Text). Spatial filing as pure metaphor — Noessel's 3D-interfaces piece flags this as visually compelling but practically inferior to keyword-search 2D.

### Pragmatic principles from designer interviews

1. **Coleran's pragmatic futurism** — one foot grounded in familiar UX hooks (parsable in 21 frames per Hojlund), other foot slightly forward. Pure-fantasy ages worse and doesn't transfer to real product. **DNA root for the entire modern FUI dialect.**
2. **Coleran's diegetic-prop discipline** — film FUI is a prop. Real software is the inverse: less detail, simpler, focused. The aesthetic-alone failure mode is shared by both.
3. **Hojlund's hero/ambient split** — pick the screens that carry story beats; lavish craft on those; everything else just needs flashing-bits-to-show-activity. Direct map to lucida priority-driven generation.
4. **Hansen's pattern-recognition over data density** — Tony Stark glances at the HUD and *knows*; he doesn't *read*. Layout is locked enough that the eye learns it. Implication for repeating substrates: maintain spatial constants across cells of the same type so the user's eye learns position semantics.
5. **Ahmed/LePore "design with purpose" / "UI is not the hero"** — every element justifies its existence narratively. Beautiful set-dressing is the failure. Character-specific aesthetic systems are how purpose manifests visually.
6. **Sheldon-Hicks NASA-realism via spreadsheets** — actual mission control runs on event-sequence spreadsheets, not dramatic visualizations. Authority comes from *correct boring data* dressed as drama, not invented drama.
7. **Bramall's dark-bg-luminous-accents as a *production* constraint**: dark bgs prevent unwanted on-set light spill. The aesthetic is secondary to a physics solve. Translatable for browser cells: dark mode lets the cell sit on any host bg without bleeding.
8. **Fraser/Hojlund "restraint > complexity"** — simplicity reads as more advanced than dense information. Inverse of intuition.
9. **GMUNK "remove greeble"** — clarity over visual complexity; conceptual readability is the goal, not detail-density.
10. **Hays "if it's good design, the point will come across in a second"** — the read-time gate is one second of camera/eye time. Lucida cells live in the same gate (scroll glance).
11. **Russell/McDermott "ideas precede tools"** — Black Mirror's variety across episodes comes from concept-first not tool-first. Substrate choice should follow concept, not be the concept.
12. **Stylow "find inspiration sideways"** — libraries / obscure books / non-FUI sources outpace Pinterest-trend FUI. Generic corporate blue is the default-failure.
13. **Booth (BLIND) "timelessness over trendiness"** — design with longevity. Avoid the dated-aesthetic trap (which currently captures most "AI-aesthetic" UI).
14. **McFetridge "do no harm"** — design for film (and by extension, for an in-conversation companion artifact) is so much about *not getting it wrong*. Consistency over visibility. Anti-FUI mode is a legitimate option when story is about technology receding.

**What the Iron Man corpus collectively highlights for lucida**:
- The HUD is a *layered system of states*, not a static composition. Every element has off / peripheral / hero modes, and transitions between them are the signature visual move.
- The cinematic-impossible 2nd-person view is a narrative construct *only*. Lucida cells can only honor 1st-person interface logic — the stereo-z-space pipeline Hansen built doesn't translate to a pane.
- JARVIS — not Tony — is the superhero. The AI is load-bearing; the user "command-and-controls." Lucida sits on the same axis: Claude is the engine, the user steers via prose.
- Cyan-on-black with red-as-alarm + all-caps tech typography is the **canonical FUI dialect**. Use as the default for "Iron-Man-mode" cells; deviate intentionally for character/faction differentiation.
- Micro-text + wireframe-detail-density that *isn't meant to be read* but rewards inspection is a deliberate design choice (Hansen, Hojlund). Translates to: render charts/diagrams with more data than the cell needs to display, so the eye finds new things on second look.

### What doesn't translate to a browser-rendered cell

- **Stock-footage compositing and 3D render-pass output** (FUI uses these heavily). Out of scope for lucida cells — no green-screen plate, no roto.
- **The 2nd-person geometrically-impossible interior view** (Iron Man HUD #4). Cinematic conceit only.
- **On-set CRT sync, tungsten color balancing, plasma off-axis visibility** (Taglianetti / Demolition Man era). Era-specific tape pipeline.
- **Stereoscopic depth-cheats and pixel-offset Z-space cheat-sheets** (Hansen Iron Man pipeline). 3D-display-medium-specific; browsers render 2D unless WebXR is in play.
- **"Live alphanumeric character churn"** as constant background — designers consistently flag it as set-dressing tell. Cheap on a browser substrate, exactly because it's cheap it reads as low-effort.
- **Embodied 3D file-system navigation** (Hackers City of Text, Johnny Mnemonic). Visually compelling, practically inferior to keyword-search 2D per Noessel's 3D-interfaces synthesis. Visual move is liftable; navigation paradigm is not.
- **Volumetric / holographic light-cast onto actors and environments** (GMUNK Tron, Avengers case-file, kimoyo beads, Doctor Strange astral). The cast-light interaction with surrounding context doesn't exist for a browser cell — the cell *is* its own canvas, no ambient room to spill into.
- **Cinematic camera-moves over the screen graphic**. FUI is built for the camera to drive eye motion across the static graphic; browser cells must drive their own motion logic.
- **Diegetic-prop "fully-functional logic even if unread"** at the depth designers describe. Hansen's micro-text passes scrutiny on Blu-ray pause — the lucida cell read-time gate is scroll-glance, much shorter. Render density should match the gate.
- **Magical-aesthetic UIs** (Doctor Strange Eye of Agamotto sigils, kimoyo bead engraved icons, Wakandan thought-control, BCI inputs). Input modality (gesture, thought, sigil-tutting) doesn't have a browser analog; the visual *output* style does.
- **CRT period-fidelity** (Alien: Earth Maginot scanlines + chromatic aberration + bulge). Reproducible via SVG filters and CSS, but expensive to maintain at high fidelity per cell. Use sparingly as a costume, not a default.
